# The Alpine Trial: A Randomized Study of Sleep Restriction

## 1. Introduction

The Alpine Trial examined whether restricting nightly sleep affects sustained
attention in healthy adults. The trial was registered before enrollment began.

## 2. Methods

The final sample consisted of 218 participants. Participants were randomly
assigned to one of two groups: a restricted-sleep group and a control group.

Data collection ran for 12 months. All sessions took place in the Marrowfield
Sleep Laboratory.

## 3. Results

The restricted-sleep group showed a 17% increase in attention lapses relative to
the control group. The difference was statistically significant.

Dropout was 9 participants, all in the restricted-sleep group.

## 4. Discussion

The authors note that the trial did not measure long-term recovery after the
restriction period ended.
